# Product

## Register

product

## Platform

web

## Users

Usul16 primarily serves seminary scholars conducting serious study of Shia hadith. Serious general readers are the secondary audience, while academic researchers should also be able to use its citations, source records, narrator data, and transmission evidence confidently. Users may be reading for long periods, locating a specific narration, comparing sources, or investigating an isnad.

## Product Purpose

Usul16 brings the major Shia hadith collections into one dependable research environment. It makes three core activities equally effortless: finding a hadith, reading a collection continuously, and investigating chains or narrators. Every experience should preserve a clear route back to the printed source so users can verify what they read.

Success means that a scholar can move naturally between text, translation, citation, narrator identity, transmission relationships, and original-source evidence without losing context or confidence.

## Positioning

A source-verifiable Shia hadith research environment for reading texts, tracing narrators, and examining transmission evidence. Coverage and editorial maturity are stated collection by collection rather than implied by a single completeness claim.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, beautiful, illuminating. Usul16 should inspire scholarly confidence while making a large and technically complex corpus feel clear, alive, and worth exploring. Its beauty should feel considered and distinctive without competing with the source material.

## Anti-references

Usul16 must not resemble a flashy or gamified app, a sterile corporate dashboard, or an antiquated religious-text website. It must avoid difficult navigation, clunky interactions, dated conventions, and any visual or behavioural detail that makes the product feel abandoned, discontinued, or unreliable.

## Design Principles

1. **Scholarship first.** Textual integrity, provenance, citation, and source verification take priority over decoration.
2. **Three equal paths.** Finding, reading, and investigating are equally important and must remain clearly reachable throughout the product.
3. **Beauty through precision.** Create visual impact through typography, composition, colour, material, and purposeful motion rather than spectacle or gimmicks.
4. **Progressive depth.** Keep the primary reading experience calm while making chains, translations, footnotes, grading, and research evidence available exactly when needed.
5. **Contemporary and enduring.** Navigation and interaction should feel current, intuitive, and maintained without chasing short-lived interface trends.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA. Support complete keyboard navigation, clear focus states, screen-reader semantics, reduced-motion preferences, strong colour contrast, comfortable touch targets, and user-adjustable Arabic reading sizes. The interface must remain usable for older scholars, younger readers, and users working through long research sessions.
